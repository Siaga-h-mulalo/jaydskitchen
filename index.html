Order Online

Order here — pay by EFT, send proof on WhatsApp

Place your order directly on this site. We'll give you an order reference, our bank details, and a WhatsApp button to send your proof of payment.

How payment works. We don't take card payments on the site. After placing your order you'll be shown our bank details and an order reference. Make an EFT, then tap the WhatsApp button to send us your proof of payment.

The 4-step flow

  • 1. Add dishes. Choose from the menu and adjust quantities.
  • 2. Fill in your details. Collection or delivery, plus your contact number.
  • 3. Get your order reference & bank details. Shown instantly after you place the order.
  • 4. Send proof of payment on WhatsApp. Use the reference as your payment description.
